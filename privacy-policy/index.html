1. Who we are

LiVEUO (“LiVEUO”, “we”, “us”) builds and supplies pharmacy software, billing applications, e-commerce platforms, custom applications, automation and digital marketing services from Bengaluru, India. We decide how and why personal data is used for the purposes in this policy, except where section 5 says we act for a customer.

This policy applies to liveuo.com, our forms, demos and business dealings with customers and prospects.

2. Data we collect

Data you give us:

  • Contact form: name, email address, company, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Downloads: name, email address and phone number, where a brochure or file asks for them.
  • Demos and secure documents: your email address and the one-time code sent to it.
  • Customers: business contact details, billing and GST details, contract records, and support requests.
  • Direct messages: anything you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Data collected automatically:

  • Pages visited, referring site, device and browser details, and approximate location, through Google Analytics.
  • IP address, processed for security and abuse prevention. We store contact and download records with a hashed version of the IP address rather than the address itself.
  • For secure documents and demo videos, an access log of the email address, IP address and time of viewing. Documents may carry this information as a visible watermark.

3. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • reply to enquiries and arrange demos or consultations;
  • provide, support and maintain software and services, and send invoices;
  • control access to demos and protected documents;
  • send service messages and, where permitted, information about our products, which you can opt out of at any time;
  • understand how the website is used, and improve it;
  • prevent spam, fraud and misuse; and
  • meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process personal data with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time, or for other purposes permitted by law, such as performing a contract or complying with a legal duty. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that has already taken place.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Essential: a session cookie that keeps secure areas working. It expires within 8 hours.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies that help us measure use of the website.
  • Security: Google reCAPTCHA, which protects our forms from automated abuse. Its use is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • Browser storage: the demo video player saves your viewing progress in your own browser.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Forms or secure areas may stop working if you do.

5. Data in our software

When a customer uses our software or asks us to host or support it, records entered into it may include personal data about that customer’s own patients, clients, staff or suppliers. The customer decides how that data is used and is responsible for having a lawful basis to use it. We handle it only as needed to provide the service, on the customer’s instructions and under our agreement.

If you are an individual whose data is held by one of our customers, such as a pharmacy, please contact that business first.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Service providers that support us, including website hosting, content delivery and security (such as Cloudflare), email delivery, analytics and anti-spam (such as Google), and messaging services;
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, auditors and lawyers;
  • Authorities where the law or a valid legal request requires it, or where needed to protect rights and safety; and
  • A successor business if LiVEUO is reorganised, merged or sold.

Some providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on the transfer being permitted by applicable law.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for: enquiries for as long as the conversation or a likely business relationship continues, customer records for the contract and any period required by tax, accounting or other law, and access logs for as long as needed for security and traceability. We then delete or anonymise it.

8. How we protect data

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls, hashed IP addresses and one-time codes for protected content. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct, complete, update or erase your data;
  • stop using your data where you withdraw consent, including for marketing;
  • address a grievance about how we handled your data; and
  • act on behalf of a person you nominate, in the event of death or incapacity.

Email info@liveuo.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and may keep data the law requires us to keep. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Other websites

Our website links to third-party sites and services, including WhatsApp and Google. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.
