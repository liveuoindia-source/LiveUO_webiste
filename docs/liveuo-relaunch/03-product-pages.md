# Remaining product pages

Same page skeleton as pharCare (problem-first hero → features grid → demo placeholder → trust →
proof → FAQ → one CTA), written shorter since pharCare carries the SEO weight. Each still ships
complete metadata, keywords, and an FAQ block — AI-answer visibility needs all product pages
covered, not just the flagship.

## Complete Pharmacy Management Software — `/products/complete-pharmacy-software`

Positioning: this is the **suite** — multi-store, warehouse, and distributor-level operations for
pharmacy groups that have outgrown single-branch billing (which is pharCare's home turf). Frame it
as "pharCare, plus the warehouse and distribution layer," not a separate unrelated product — buyers
researching one will look for the other.

### SEO metadata
- **Meta title:** Complete Pharmacy Management Software | liveuo
- **Meta description:** Multi-store pharmacy management software covering warehouse stock, distributor billing, and branch-level reporting. Built for pharmacy chains. Book a demo.
- **Canonical:** `/products/complete-pharmacy-software`

### Keywords
- Branded: `liveuo pharmacy suite`, `liveuo pharmacy management software`
- Category: `pharmacy management software`, `pharmacy chain management software`, `pharmacy warehouse management software`
- Long-tail: `software for multi-store pharmacy chains India`, `pharmacy distributor billing software`

### H1/H2
```
H1  Pharmacy management for chains that outgrew single-branch billing
H2  Built for the layer above the counter
H2  What's included
H2  See it in action
H2  Works with pharCare, or on its own
H2  Frequently asked questions
H2  Talk to us about your chain
```

### Copy notes
- Hero problem: head office losing visibility once you're past 3-4 branches — consolidated
  reporting, warehouse stock, and distributor purchasing become the bottleneck, not counter billing.
- Features grid: Central warehouse inventory · Distributor/supplier billing · Branch performance
  reporting · Consolidated GST reporting across branches · Role-based access for regional managers
  · Demand forecasting/reorder automation.
- FAQ:
  - **How is this different from pharCare?** pharCare runs billing at the counter; the Complete
    Pharmacy Management Software adds the warehouse, distributor, and multi-branch reporting layer
    on top — most chains run both together.

    dependency/integration model before publishing this answer]`
