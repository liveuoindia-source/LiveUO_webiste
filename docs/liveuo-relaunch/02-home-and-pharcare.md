### Companion page — `/products/pharcare-vs-marg-erp`

A dedicated comparison landing page captures competitive-intent search ("pharCare vs Marg",
"Marg ERP alternative") without cluttering the main pharCare page with a named competitor. Use:

- **Meta title:** pharCare vs Marg ERP — Pharmacy Billing Software Comparison
- **H1:** pharCare vs. Marg ERP: which pharmacy billing software fits your pharmacy?
- Structure: neutral comparison table (deployment model, multi-branch sync, GST invoicing,
  Schedule H handling, support model, pricing model) followed by "why pharmacies switch from Marg
  to pharCare" — written from real migration reasons your sales team hears, not assumptions.
- **Do not publish claims about Marg's specific feature set that your team hasn't verified
  firsthand** — a factually wrong comparison table is a reputational and legal risk. Verify each
  row before publishing.
