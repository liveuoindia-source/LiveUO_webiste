Results and measurement

The metrics we report on

Agreed at the start, tracked throughout, and explained in plain language.

Website traffic

How many people visit and where they come from. Useful only alongside what they then do.

Search rankings & organic visibility

Where your pages appear for target searches, and how many impressions and clicks that earns.

Click-through rate (CTR)

The share of people who saw an ad or listing and clicked. A signal of relevance.

Cost per lead (CPL)

Total spend divided by leads generated. Compare it with lead quality, not in isolation.

Conversion rate

The share of visitors who complete the action you want, such as an enquiry or purchase.

Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

Everything spent on marketing and sales to win one new customer.

Return on ad spend (ROAS)

Revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend. It does not account for margin.

Return on investment (ROI)

Profit generated relative to total marketing cost, including fees and tools.

Customer lifetime value (CLV)

What a customer is worth over the whole relationship; it sets how much you can afford to pay to win one.

No guaranteed numbers. Results depend on your industry, budget, competition and how well a campaign is executed. We set realistic targets after an audit, and tell you when a channel is not working.

Sales spike during offers and fall away afterwards, and most buyers never return.
Strategy
Shift spend from one-time acquisition to repeat purchase.
Execution
Post-purchase email and WhatsApp flows, a replenishment reminder timed to product life, and catalogue ads that exclude recent buyers.
What we would measure
Repeat purchase rate, share of revenue from existing customers, and ROAS after discounts.
Illustrative example · Manufacturing

An equipment maker reliant on trade fairs

Challenge
Almost all enquiries arrive at two annual exhibitions, leaving the sales team idle in between.
Strategy
Make the catalogue discoverable to engineers searching by specification.
Execution
Product pages with datasheets, application notes by industry, and LinkedIn campaigns to purchase and plant heads in target regions.
What we would measure
RFQs per month, enquiries from new regions, and datasheet downloads.
