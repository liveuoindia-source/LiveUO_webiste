The problem

What pharCare replaces.

Most pharmacies run billing on a paper or Excel dispensing register, with each branch billing in isolation and GST returns pieced together by hand at month-end. That's what pharCare replaces — not by digitizing the same workflow, but by removing the three failure points inside it: manual billing errors, expired stock nobody caught in time, and branches that don't know what each other has in stock.

What's included

Everything a modern pharmacy counter needs.

Billing & Invoicing

Barcode and quick-key billing with GST-compliant invoices generated automatically on every sale.

Batch & Expiry Tracking

Every SKU tracked by batch number and expiry date, with near-expiry alerts before stock becomes a write-off.

Multi-Branch Stock Sync

Real-time inventory visibility across every branch from a single dashboard — no end-of-day file exports.

Schedule H / H1 / H1X Compliance

Controlled and prescription-only drug sales are logged and flagged automatically to match Indian drug-schedule rules.

GST Returns-Ready Reports

Sales and purchase data exported in the format your CA needs for GSTR filing, without manual reconciliation.

Supplier & Purchase Orders

Reorder points, supplier ledgers, and purchase tracking in the same system as billing.

Role-Based Staff Access

Cashiers, pharmacists, and branch managers each see only what their role needs.

Data Migration

Stock, batch, and customer records imported from most existing pharmacy billing systems, including Marg ERP, during onboarding.
