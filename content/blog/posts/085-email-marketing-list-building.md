Social media reach changes when platforms change their algorithms. Ad costs rise. An email list, built with consent, is something you own: a direct line to people who asked to hear from you.

For many small businesses, it's one of the highest-return marketing channels — and one of the most neglected.

## Grow the list with consent

Offer something people value in return for their email:

- **e-commerce:** first-order discount, back-in-stock alerts, early access to sales
- **B2B:** checklists, guides, templates, price lists
- **services:** useful tips, booking reminders, seasonal advice

Place sign-ups where interested people are:

- website pop-up or banner (not intrusive)
- checkout, with an unticked opt-in box
- end of blog articles
- at events and in-store, with a QR code

Be clear what they'll receive and how often. Record when and how each person subscribed.

## What to send

### Newsletters

Useful content, not just promotions:

- tips related to your products or services
- new products or services
- customer stories (with permission)
- relevant offers

A useful ratio for many businesses: mostly helpful content, with occasional offers.

### Automated emails

These work in the background and often perform best:

| Automation | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Welcome series | New subscriber |
| Abandoned cart | Cart left without purchase |
| Post-purchase | Order delivered — tips, review request |
| Reorder reminder | When a consumable is likely to run out |
| Win-back | No purchase for a set period |

## Write emails that get read

- a clear, specific subject line
- one main message per email
- short paragraphs, readable on a phone
- one clear call to action
- your name or business name as the sender, consistently

## Segment

Even simple segments improve results:

- customers vs subscribers who haven't bought
- product categories of interest
- location
- recent vs lapsed customers

## Deliverability basics

To reach inboxes rather than spam folders:

- set up domain authentication — SPF, DKIM and DMARC
- use a reputable email service
- remove addresses that bounce
- make unsubscribing easy
- avoid sudden large sends to old, unengaged lists

## Measure

- clicks and click rate
- sales or enquiries from email
- unsubscribes and spam complaints
- list growth

Open rates are less reliable than they used to be; focus on actions.

## Email and WhatsApp together

Use each for what it does best: WhatsApp for short, timely messages and conversations; email for longer content, receipts, newsletters and B2B communication. Let customers choose their preference.

## Start this month

1. Add a sign-up form with a clear offer.
2. Write a three-email welcome series.
3. Send one useful newsletter.
4. Set up one automation — abandoned cart or post-purchase.

An email list grows slowly, but every subscriber is an audience you can reach without paying for each message. Over time, that becomes one of your most valuable assets.
