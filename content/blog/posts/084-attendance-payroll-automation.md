Month-end payroll in many small businesses still looks like this: attendance printed from the biometric machine, leave applications collected on paper or WhatsApp, overtime noted in a register, and someone spending two days in Excel working out each salary. Errors are common, and every one becomes an awkward conversation.

Attendance and payroll are well suited to automation because the rules, once agreed, apply the same way every month.

## The flow

1. **Attendance** captured by biometric device, mobile check-in or system login.
2. **Shifts and rules** applied — late marks, half days, overtime, weekly offs, holidays.
3. **Leave** applied for and approved in the system.
4. **Payable days** calculated per employee.
5. **Salary** calculated — earnings, deductions, reimbursements.
6. **Review and approval** of exceptions.
7. **Payslips and payments** generated.

## Agree the rules first

Automation needs rules written down:

- shift timings and grace period
- what counts as late, half day or absent
- overtime eligibility and rates
- leave types, entitlements and carry-forward
- holidays by location
- how missing punches are handled

Many disputes come from rules that were never written down. Agreeing them is half the benefit.

## Attendance capture

- **Biometric devices** — sync data automatically rather than exporting manually.
- **Mobile attendance** for field staff, with location or photo check-in and clear privacy notices.
- **Regularisation** — employees request correction for missed punches; managers approve.

## Leave management

- apply, approve and track leave in the system
- show balances to employees
- apply approved leave automatically to payroll

## Payroll calculation

- earnings components per employee
- statutory deductions as applicable — such as PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS — based on current rules
- loans, advances and reimbursements
- arrears and one-time payments

Keep statutory rules updated and review them with your accountant, since rates and thresholds change.

## Review exceptions, not everything

Instead of checking every salary, review:

- employees with unusual attendance
- large differences from last month
- missing data
- manual adjustments

Then approve and release.

## Payslips and records

Generate payslips automatically and share them securely. Keep records of attendance, leave and payroll for the period the law requires.

## Benefits

- payroll processing time cut dramatically
- consistent application of rules
- fewer disputes, because employees can see their attendance and leave
- reports for management — overtime, absenteeism, cost by department

## Data privacy

Attendance, location and salary data are personal data. Limit access to those who need it, protect it properly, and be transparent with employees about what is collected and why.

## Starting point

1. Write down attendance and leave rules.
2. Connect attendance devices to one system.
3. Move leave applications into the system.
4. Run automated payroll in parallel with the manual method for one or two months.
5. Switch over once the numbers match.

Automated payroll won't remove the need for judgment, but it removes the arithmetic — and gives everyone a payroll they can trust.
