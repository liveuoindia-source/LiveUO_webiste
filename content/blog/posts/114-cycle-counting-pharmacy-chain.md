Annual stock-takes in a pharmacy are painful: the store closes or runs short-staffed, counting goes late into the night, and by the time the differences are found, nobody remembers what caused them. In a chain, multiply that by every branch.

Cycle counting replaces one big count with many small ones, done as part of normal work.

## How it works

Instead of counting everything once a year, each branch counts a small part of its stock regularly:

- a few racks each week
- one category each week
- high-value and controlled items every week or month

Over a cycle — a quarter, for example — every item gets counted at least once.

## Decide what to count, and how often

| Group | Examples | Suggested frequency |
|---|---|---|
| High value | Costly injectables, specialty medicines | Weekly |
| Controlled | Schedule H1, X, narcotics | Weekly or as required |
| Fast movers | Top-selling items | Monthly |
| Regular items | Most of the range | Quarterly |
| Slow movers | Rarely sold items | Quarterly or half-yearly |

## Count properly

- count from a **system-generated count sheet**, sorted by rack location — see [rack location mapping](/resources/blog/pharmacy-rack-location-mapping/)
- **don't show expected quantities** on the sheet, so counters count rather than confirm
- count **by batch and expiry**, not just item
- count at a quiet time, and pause billing for that rack if possible

## Investigate differences

For each difference above a threshold:

1. **recount**
2. **check recent transactions** — sales, returns, transfers, purchases
3. **check batches** — stock entered under a wrong batch often shows as a gain on one and a loss on another
4. **identify the cause**
5. **adjust** with a reason and approval

## Common causes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sold under wrong item or batch | Training, barcode scanning |
| Transfers not received in system | Receipt confirmation — see [inter-branch transfers](/resources/blog/inter-branch-stock-transfer-pharmacy/) |
| Returns not entered | Return process discipline |
| Free goods not entered | Purchase entry checks |
| Breakage not recorded | Breakage recording routine |
| Theft | Controls, cameras, audit trail review |

The value of cycle counting is that causes are found while recent enough to trace.

## Chain-level oversight

Head office should see, by branch:

- counts completed versus planned
- difference value and percentage
- adjustments and their reasons
- recurring problem items

Branches with persistent differences need support or closer review.

## Benefits

- stock figures stay accurate all year
- problems found within days, not months
- no store closures for counting
- better purchasing decisions, because system stock can be trusted

## Getting started

1. Group items by value, control and movement.
2. Set a counting calendar for each branch.
3. Generate count sheets from the system.
4. Review differences weekly.
5. Track accuracy by branch over time.

Accurate stock is the foundation of every other decision in a pharmacy chain. Cycle counting keeps it accurate with less disruption than any annual count.
