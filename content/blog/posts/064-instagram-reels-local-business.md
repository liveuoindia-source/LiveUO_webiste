Short-form video has become one of the main ways people discover local businesses — a café, a clinic, a boutique, a repair shop. A good Reel shows what you do, who does it, and why someone should choose you, in less than a minute.

You don't need a studio or a big budget. You need a plan, a phone, and consistency.

## What works for local businesses

The most effective content usually falls into a few types:

### Behind the scenes

How the product is made, how the service is delivered, how the shop gets ready in the morning. It builds trust because it shows real people and real work.

### Before and after

Ideal for salons, cleaning, interiors, repairs, landscaping and fitness. Show the transformation, with honest results.

### Answer a common question

"How long does a screen replacement take?" "Which cake is best for a kids' party?" Each answer is useful, searchable and shows expertise.

### Meet the team

Short introductions to the people customers will meet. Especially effective for clinics, coaching centres and service businesses.

### Customer stories

Short testimonials, with the customer's permission. Real customers speaking are more convincing than any caption.

### Local relevance

Festivals, local events, weather, neighbourhood landmarks. It tells the algorithm and the viewer where you are.

## Filming on a phone

- **Vertical**, filling the screen.
- **Natural light** — face a window rather than standing in front of one.
- **Clear sound** — a simple clip-on mic makes a big difference.
- **Hook in the first two seconds** — show the result, ask a question, or start with movement.
- **Captions on screen** — many people watch without sound.
- **Short** — most local business Reels work best under 30–45 seconds.

## A simple weekly rhythm

| Day | Content type |
|---|---|
| Monday | Answer a customer question |
| Wednesday | Behind the scenes or before/after |
| Friday | Team, customer story or local tie-in |

Batch-film several on one quiet afternoon. Edit with the built-in tools or a simple app.

## Turn views into enquiries

Views are not the goal. Enquiries are.

- Put your **location, hours and contact link** in your profile.
- Add a **WhatsApp or booking link** in the bio.
- End Reels with a simple call to action: "Book on WhatsApp — link in bio."
- Reply to comments and DMs quickly.
- Pin your best three Reels to the profile.

## Boost selectively

When a Reel performs well organically, consider boosting it to people within a few kilometres. Organic performance is a good signal of what will work as an ad.

## Measure

Each month, check:

- reach and views — is the audience growing?
- profile visits and link clicks
- DMs and WhatsApp messages from Instagram
- new customers who mention Instagram

Double down on the content types that bring messages, not just likes.

## Mistakes to avoid

- posting only offers and price lists
- using trending audio that has nothing to do with your business
- inconsistent posting
- ignoring comments
- using music or footage you don't have rights to

Reels reward businesses that show up regularly and honestly. A local business that posts useful, real content every week for six months usually becomes the one people in the area recognise first.
