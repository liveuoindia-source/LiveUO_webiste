Billing software sits at the centre of a small business. Every sale passes through it, and its data feeds GST returns, stock, accounts and decisions. Choosing badly means months of workarounds or a painful second switch.

Here's what to check before you buy.

## 1. GST compliance

Non-negotiable:

- correct tax invoices, bills of supply and credit/debit notes
- HSN/SAC codes on item masters
- CGST/SGST vs IGST based on place of supply
- invoice numbering that follows GST rules
- GSTR-1 data ready to file or export
- e-invoicing and e-way bill support if you need them now or may later

Ask the vendor how quickly they update the software when rules change.

## 2. Speed where it matters

If you bill at a counter, test it:

- how many seconds to create a typical bill?
- barcode scanning
- keyboard shortcuts, not just mouse clicks
- quick customer lookup by phone number
- multiple payment modes on one bill

A few seconds per bill adds up across hundreds of bills a day.

## 3. Inventory

Does stock update with each sale and purchase? Can it handle batches, expiry, serial numbers or variants if your products need them? Are there low-stock alerts?

## 4. Multiple users and locations

- separate logins and permissions
- audit trail of edits and cancellations
- support for multiple counters or branches, if you have or plan them

## 5. Offline and online

If your internet is unreliable, make sure billing keeps working offline and syncs safely when connected. If you want to check sales from home, you'll need cloud access.

## 6. Reports

At minimum: daily sales by payment mode, GST summaries, stock levels, outstanding receivables, and sales by item and customer. Check reports can be exported to Excel or PDF.

## 7. Integrations

Do you need it to work with your accounting software, payment terminals, e-commerce store, WhatsApp or SMS? Ask exactly how — automatic sync, or manual export?

## 8. Data ownership and backups

- automatic, regular backups
- easy restore
- full data export in a standard format
- clarity on what happens to your data if you stop paying

## 9. Support

- support hours and channels — phone, WhatsApp, email
- language
- response time for urgent issues
- training for staff
- local presence, if on-site help matters

## 10. Total cost

Look beyond the headline price:

| Cost | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| Licence | One-time or subscription? Per user, per branch? |
| Setup | Data migration, configuration, training |
| Updates | Included, or paid? |
| Support | Included, or annual fee? |
| Hardware | Printers, scanners, terminals |
| Growth | Cost of adding users or branches |

## Try before you commit

Ask for a trial or demo with your own data — a few real items, customers and typical bills. Let the person who bills every day test it, not just the owner.

## Red flags

- vague answers about GST updates
- no way to export your data
- one shared login for everyone
- "we'll customise it later" for core needs
- support only by email with long response times

The right billing software quietly does its job for years. Spending an extra week on evaluation is a small price for that.
