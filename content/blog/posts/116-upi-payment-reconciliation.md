UPI has made payments effortless for customers. For businesses, it's created a quiet reconciliation problem: payments arrive in the bank or merchant app, while bills are recorded in the billing software, and the two don't always agree.

At the end of the month, an unexplained difference between UPI received and UPI billed is very hard to trace. At the end of the day, it's usually easy.

## Why they drift apart

- **wrong payment mode** — a UPI payment saved as cash, or the reverse
- **failed payment, bill saved** — the customer showed a screen, but the payment didn't go through
- **payment without a bill** — an advance, a settlement, or a mistake
- **partial or excess payment** — customer paid a different amount
- **refunds** sent by UPI but not recorded
- **settlement timing** — some merchant accounts settle in batches

## Static vs dynamic QR

| | Static QR | Dynamic QR / payment link |
|---|---|---|
| Amount | Customer enters | Fixed per bill |
| Link to bill | Manual | Automatic |
| Errors | More common | Fewer |
| Reconciliation | Match by amount and time | Often automatic |

Where possible, generate a QR or payment link for the specific bill amount from your billing software. The payment is then linked to the bill, and status can update automatically.

## A daily routine

1. **Print or view the day's UPI sales** from the billing software, by counter and user.
2. **Download the day's UPI credits** from the merchant app or bank.
3. **Match by amount and time.** Most will match one-to-one.
4. **List unmatched items** on both sides.
5. **Investigate** — check bills around the same time, ask the counter staff, check for wrong payment modes.
6. **Correct** — fix payment modes, record missing receipts, follow up failed payments with the customer.
7. **Record the result** — matched total, differences and reasons.

It takes minutes when done daily.

## Rules that prevent problems

- **Never save a UPI bill until the payment is confirmed** — on the merchant device or app, not just the customer's screen.
- **One payment mode per bill line**, or split payments recorded properly.
- **Refunds recorded in the system** with the UPI reference.
- **Credit customers' UPI payments** recorded against invoices with the reference number.

## Software features that help

- dynamic QR or payment links per bill
- automatic status update from the payment provider
- split payments across modes
- day-end report by payment mode and user
- import of bank or merchant statements for matching

## Measure

Track daily: UPI billed, UPI received, matched, and unexplained difference. The unexplained figure should be zero most days; when it isn't, the routine finds out why.

UPI makes collecting money easy. A ten-minute daily match makes sure every rupee collected is the rupee you billed.
