Meta lead ads — lead forms on Facebook and Instagram — can generate enquiries very cheaply. Many businesses then discover why: half the numbers don't answer, some people don't remember filling the form, and a few leads are clearly not real buyers.

Cheap leads aren't valuable if they don't become customers. Lead quality can be improved substantially with a few changes.

## Why quality suffers

- **One-tap submission.** Forms pre-fill name and phone, so submitting takes almost no effort.
- **Broad targeting.** The platform finds people likely to submit forms, not necessarily likely to buy.
- **Vague offers.** "Get more information" attracts the curious.
- **Slow follow-up.** By the time someone calls, interest has faded.

## Improve the form

### Add qualifying questions

One or two questions that filter:

- "Which service are you interested in?" (multiple choice)
- "When are you looking to start?"
- "What is your budget range?"
- "Which area are you in?"

Each question reduces volume slightly and improves quality.

### Use the higher-intent option

Meta offers a form type with a review step before submission. It adds friction on purpose.

### Be specific in the intro

State what the person is signing up for, including price guidance where possible: "Get a quote for rooftop solar — systems from ₹X". People who wouldn't pay that range self-select out.

### Set expectations

"Our team will call you within 30 minutes" tells people a call is coming.

## Follow up fast

Speed matters more for lead ads than almost any other channel:

- connect the form to your CRM or WhatsApp so leads arrive instantly
- send an automatic WhatsApp acknowledgement
- call within minutes during business hours
- try more than once, at different times

See [automating lead follow-up](/resources/blog/lead-follow-up-automation/).

## Feed results back

Tell Meta which leads were good:

- mark leads as qualified, converted or junk in your CRM
- share these outcomes using Meta's conversion tools

The platform then optimises towards people who become customers, not just form-fillers.

## Targeting and creative

- target locations you actually serve
- show the product or service clearly in the creative
- avoid clickbait headlines that attract the wrong audience

## Measure what matters

Track per campaign:

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead | Volume efficiency |
| Contact rate | Are leads real and reachable? |
| Qualified rate | Are they the right people? |
| Cost per qualified lead | The real cost |
| Cost per customer | The final test |

A campaign with twice the cost per lead but three times the qualified rate is the better campaign.

## When to choose a landing page instead

For higher-value services, sending people to a landing page with a form often brings fewer but better leads, because it takes more effort and gives more information. Test both. See [building a landing page for ads](/resources/blog/landing-page-for-ads/).

Lead ads can be an excellent channel. The trick is to stop optimising for the cheapest lead and start optimising for the lead most likely to become a customer.
