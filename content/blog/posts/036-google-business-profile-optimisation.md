For a local business, the Google Business Profile is often seen more than the website. When someone searches "pharmacy near me", "CA in Jayanagar" or "laptop repair Banashankari", the map results with their phone numbers, hours and reviews appear first — and many people never scroll past them.

The profile is free. Most businesses still leave it half finished. That's an opportunity.

## How local results are ranked

Google says local results depend mainly on three things:

- **Relevance** — how well the profile matches the search
- **Distance** — how close the business is to the searcher or the place searched
- **Prominence** — how well known the business is, including reviews, links and overall web presence

You can't move your shop closer to everyone. You can control relevance completely, and influence prominence steadily.

## The setup checklist

### Name, address and phone

- Use your **real business name** exactly as on your signboard. Don't add keywords or locations — it's against Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.
- Use the **same address and phone number** everywhere: website, social profiles, directories. Consistency helps Google trust the listing.
- Use a phone number that is answered during business hours.

### Categories

The **primary category** is one of the strongest relevance signals. Choose the most specific one that describes your main business — "Pharmacy" rather than "Store", "Chartered Accountant" rather than "Consultant". Add secondary categories only for services you genuinely offer.

### Hours

Set regular hours and add special hours for festivals and holidays. A customer who arrives to a closed shutter after Google said "open" doesn't come back — and often leaves a review about it.

### Description, services and products

Write a plain description of what you do and whom you serve. Add services and products with short descriptions and, where it makes sense, prices. These help the profile match more specific searches.

### Photos

Add real photos: the storefront from the street (so people recognise it), the interior, the team, and your products or work. Update them periodically. Profiles with current photos look active and trustworthy.

### Booking and messaging

If customers can book or enquire online, add the booking link. Enable calls and messages only if someone will actually answer them promptly.

## Reviews: the part that keeps working

Reviews influence both ranking and the customer's decision. Build them steadily:

- **Ask every satisfied customer.** A printed QR code at the counter and a link in the follow-up message work well.
- **Never buy or fake reviews**, and don't offer rewards in exchange for positive ones. It breaks Google's policies and erodes trust when it's spotted.
- **Reply to every review**, good and bad. Thank people for positive ones; for negative ones, acknowledge the issue, avoid arguing, and offer to resolve it.

A steady flow of recent reviews is worth more than a burst of many in one month followed by silence.

## Keep the profile active

- **Posts.** Share offers, new services, festival hours or useful tips. They keep the profile fresh.
- **Questions and answers.** Add common questions yourself with clear answers — parking, home delivery, accepted payments.
- **Updates.** Change hours, phone numbers and services the day they change.

## Track what it brings

The profile shows how many people viewed it, called, requested directions or visited your website. Track these monthly. If calls fall while views stay steady, check your hours, photos and recent reviews. If views fall, check categories and whether a competitor has become more active.

For the website clicks, add tracking parameters to the website link so your analytics can separate Google Business Profile visits from other search traffic.

## Common mistakes

- Keywords stuffed into the business name
- Two profiles for the same location
- Old phone numbers and outdated hours
- No photos, or only the logo
- Ignoring negative reviews for weeks

## A monthly routine

Fifteen minutes a month keeps a profile healthy:

1. Reply to new reviews and questions.
2. Add two or three new photos.
3. Publish one post.
4. Check hours, especially before holidays.
5. Note calls, direction requests and website clicks.

It is one of the cheapest marketing channels a local business has. The businesses that win in local results are rarely the biggest — they're the ones that keep their profile complete, current and full of genuine reviews.
