For an online store, the courier is the part of the customer experience you control least and customers judge most. A late, damaged or failed delivery is remembered as the store's failure, not the courier's.

Choosing shipping partners deserves more than comparing rate cards.

## What to compare

### Coverage

Can they deliver to the pin codes where your customers are? Check serviceability for prepaid and COD separately — some areas are prepaid only.

### Delivered rate

The share of shipments delivered successfully. Ask for numbers by region, and measure it yourself after launch. This is the single most important metric.

### Speed

Typical delivery times by zone — within city, within state, metro to metro, rest of India. Customers increasingly expect clear, short delivery windows.

### COD handling

- COD charges
- remittance time — how many days after delivery you receive the money
- remittance reports you can reconcile

### Returns and RTO

- reverse pickup availability and cost
- RTO charges
- how quickly returned shipments reach you
- condition of returned goods

### Integration

- automatic order sync from your store
- label generation and pickup scheduling
- tracking updates pushed to customers
- webhooks or APIs for status

### Support

- how disputes about lost or damaged shipments are handled
- weight discrepancy resolution
- an account manager at higher volumes

## Aggregator or direct?

| | Aggregator | Direct courier |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Quick | Contract and onboarding |
| Courier choice | Many, through one panel | One per contract |
| Rates | Good at low volume | Better at high volume |
| Routing | Rules across couriers | Manual unless you build it |
| Support | Through the aggregator | Directly with the courier |

Many stores start with an aggregator and add direct contracts for their highest-volume lanes later.

## Calculate real cost

Rate per shipment isn't the cost. Calculate **cost per delivered order**:

- forward shipping
- COD charges
- RTO costs, both ways
- weight discrepancy charges
- damaged or lost shipments not reimbursed

A courier that's cheaper per shipment but has more RTO can cost more per delivered order.

## Packaging and weight

Couriers charge by actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher. Right-size your packaging, record weights accurately, and keep photos of packed parcels — weight disputes are common.

## Keep customers informed

Send tracking links and status updates by WhatsApp or email: shipped, out for delivery, delivered. Proactive updates reduce "where is my order?" messages and failed deliveries.

## Review monthly

Track by courier and region:

- delivered rate
- average delivery time
- RTO rate
- damage and loss claims
- weight disputes

Shift volume towards partners that perform, and renegotiate with data.

Shipping is where online promises meet real roads. The right partners — measured on delivered orders, not rate cards — make your store look reliable. See also [reducing RTO and returns](/resources/blog/reduce-rto-returns-ecommerce/).
