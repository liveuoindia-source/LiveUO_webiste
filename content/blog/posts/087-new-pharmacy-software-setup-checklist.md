A new pharmacy has a hundred things to arrange before opening day. Software setup often gets squeezed into the last evening — and then the first week is spent fixing wrong tax rates, missing batches and bills that don't print properly.

A few hours of careful setup before opening saves weeks of corrections.

## 1. Business details

- legal name and trade name
- address and contact details
- GSTIN and GST registration type
- drug licence numbers (retail licences as applicable)
- registered pharmacist's name and registration number
- financial year start

These appear on every bill, so check them carefully.

## 2. Item master

- import or select items from a medicine database
- check each item's name, manufacturer, composition and pack size
- set **HSN code and GST rate** — the most important fields to get right
- mark **scheduled drugs** (H, H1, X) correctly
- mark **cold-chain items**
- set rack or shelf location if you'll use it
- set reorder levels for fast movers (you can refine later)

## 3. Suppliers

- name, address, GSTIN and drug licence number for each distributor
- contact person, credit terms

## 4. Customers

- credit customers with limits and credit days
- clinics or institutions you'll supply
- loyalty programme settings, if any

## 5. Opening stock

Enter every item on the shelf with:

- batch number
- expiry date
- MRP
- purchase cost
- quantity

This is the biggest job. Do it by shelf, double-check expiry dates, and don't skip batch details — they're essential for expiry and recall tracking from day one.

## 6. Users and permissions

- individual login for every staff member
- roles for counter, pharmacist, purchase and manager
- discount limits and approval rules

See [user roles in pharmacy software](/resources/blog/pharmacy-user-roles-permissions/).

## 7. Billing settings

- bill numbering series with the financial year
- bill format and printed fields
- payment modes — cash, UPI, cards, credit
- rounding rules
- prescription capture for scheduled drugs

## 8. Hardware

- bill printer — test print size and alignment
- barcode scanner
- cash drawer
- UPI QR display or card terminal

Print test bills until everything looks right.

## 9. Backups

- confirm automatic backups are working
- know how to restore
- if you use cloud software, confirm offline billing and sync

## 10. Test a full day

Before opening, run through a complete day in a test mode:

- purchase entry with free quantity
- sale with a scheduled drug and prescription
- sale with credit and UPI
- sales return
- day-end report

## After opening

- review the first week's reports for errors
- adjust item details as you find issues
- refine reorder levels after a month of sales

A careful setup means the first bill is right, the first GST return is right, and the first stock audit holds no surprises.
