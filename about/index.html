Software engineers who specialize, not generalize.

LiVEUO was founded on a simple idea: teams that go deep in one domain build better software than teams that spread thin across many.

Our story

Nine years, four disciplines, one standard

We started building e-commerce storefronts in 2017. A pharmacy client asked us to digitize their dispensing log in 2019 — and that engagement became pharCare, once we saw how much better software gets when the team actually understands the regulatory and operational weight of the domain.

Today pharCare is our flagship product, alongside the Complete Pharmacy Management Software, an e-commerce platform, a billing application, and custom application development — all under one engineering standard: documented, tested, and built to outlive the person who wrote it.
